## 1. Price: 0.99, and the ambiguity

The request said "0,99". Interpreted as **0.99 in the main currency (0.99 USD / EUR), not 0.99 cents**. Microsoft's price tiers start at 0.99 USD (then 1.09, 1.19...), so 0.99 cents cannot be set at all (source: "Set app pricing for MSIX app", Microsoft Learn). If the owner meant something else, only this section and the listings' "About this edition" line change.

## 2. Why paid, and what to say about the free build

Facts the listing must keep true (policy 10.1.1, 10.8.4):
- The Store edition is **not** a superset of the free build. It leaves out executable downloads, the torrent engine and game-archive downloads, and ships no model files. The listing says so.
- The free build (Portable / MSI on GitHub) has the same source. The project is source-available (FSL-1.1-MIT); the README says "free, no required subscription or paid feature unlocks". The Store listing therefore must not imply the paid edition unlocks features. It sells convenience and support: installation, automatic updates and clean uninstall through the Store, and a way to support development.
- Do not call the Store edition "Pro" or "Premium" and do not call the other build "limited" or "trial".

## 3. Free trial

Recommendation: **No free trial.** Reasons: (a) the code has no Store license check (no `StoreContext`/trial logic in `src/`), so a trial would be only a Store-side time window with no in-app handling; verify before enabling; (b) the free GitHub build already serves as the way to try the software; (c) an "Unlimited" trial needs the app to limit features, which would contradict "no paid feature unlocks". Partner Center offers Time-limited (1, 7, 15 or 30 days) or Unlimited trials. Status: SERVE DECISIONE (default: none).

## 6. Order of purchase in a paid app (what policy says)

For a paid (up-front price) app the customer buys through the Store before installing; there is **no in-app purchase flow, no payment screen and no purchase API call** in PaRuX. That keeps PaRuX outside policy 10.8 (financial transactions apply to apps with in-app purchases, subscriptions, billing or financial data). Consequence for the build: the app must stay free of any "buy", "upgrade", "unlock" or "pay" control. See `POLICY_AND_CERTIFICATION.md` section 2 for donations and external links.
